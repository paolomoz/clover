Executive summary

Three P1s,
one lever

The single biggest lever is finishing the new-template migration across the purchase path and putting representative prices on /pricing: those two moves resolve two of the three P1 findings between them and de-risk the site’s highest-intent traffic. Three findings carry P1 severity: the pricing page withholds prices at the moment of maximum anxiety (F-001); two visual template generations ship simultaneously on the core purchase path (F-002); and mobile main-thread blocking is poor, with lab TBT at 1,124 ms against a 600 ms threshold (F-003). Performance (40) and content & LLM visibility (48) are the two weakest dimensions, while brand expression (72) is the strongest; the acid-lime system captured on the home page is distinctive and worth consolidating rather than replacing. Everything else in this report is detail in service of those three moves.

Stated assumptions

  1. Primary conversion action assumed to be assisted sales (“Contact sales” — the most repeated CTA, 11 occurrences on all 5 pages) with self-serve “Shop systems” as the secondary path.
  2. A same-day 5-page extraction was reused instead of re-crawling at the default 8-page audit cap; the 5 pages cover five distinct templates (landing, product pillar, solution, pricing, contact).
  3. The home page <title> targeting the Food & Beverage vertical is assumed to be the default state of a personalization system; the audit assesses the page as served to a first-time visitor.
  4. impeccable audit-arm checks (contrast, responsive overflow, reduced-motion, alt coverage, CWV) were computed directly with the same detector bundle + Playwright instead of a second sub-skill invocation; the critique arm ran as the mandated dual-agent pass (26/40).

The split

The defining fact of the brand surface is that two visual template generations ship simultaneously. The home page runs the new system (PP Formula Condensed 92px uppercase display, acid-lime/black buttons, a 1280–1392px container, mark-only logo) while pricing and pos-systems run the classic one: Altform sentence-case H1s, #228800 buttons, a 1158px container, wordmark logo. The split is documented as finding F-002 and pictured in Figure 1 (§4). The design-token layer that would make consolidation cheap is vestigial: one CSS custom property site-wide (F-013).

Findings ledger

13 findings: 3 at P1, 7 at P2, 3 at P3, ordered by severity. Each carries its observed evidence, business impact, and a concrete fix. Screenshot evidence is embedded as captioned figures.

F-001 P1 conversion-focus

The pricing page withholds prices at the moment of maximum anxiety

Evidence
One teaser rate (“Pay as little as 2.3% + 10¢ per transaction”) and a 6-way business-type self-classification; no device costs, no plan table on the page. Buyer must self-classify before seeing any number. screenshot — stardust/current/assets/screenshots/pricing.png, hero + router region · Figure 2
Impact
Comparison shoppers bounce to Square, whose pricing page answers in one screen; the highest-intent visitors hit the least reassuring page.
Fix
Surface a representative anchor price per segment on /pricing (e.g. “Full-service dining from $X/mo + hardware from $Y”), keep the router for refinement, and move the phone + “Schedule a call” block above the feature cards.
F-002 P1 visual-hierarchy-craft

Two visual template generations ship simultaneously on the core purchase path

Evidence
Home: PP Formula Condensed 92px uppercase display, acid-lime/black buttons, 1280–1392px container, mark-only logo. Pricing/pos-systems: Altform sentence-case H1s, #228800 buttons, 1158px container, wordmark logo. Critique heuristic “Consistency & standards” scored 1/4. screenshot — home.png vs pos-systems.png/pricing.png (headers, buttons, H1 typography) · Figure 1, Figure 2
Impact
A high-stakes buyer doing 10-tab comparison shopping reads the inconsistency as organizational churn exactly where money changes hands; it also splits the design system into two maintenance targets.
Fix
Port the new-template header/CTA/footer tokens site-wide as a skin before migrating layouts; sequence full migration by funnel order (pricing and pos-systems first — one click from the hero CTAs).
F-003 P1 performance

Mobile main-thread blocking is poor: lab TBT 1,124 ms (threshold: 600 ms)

Evidence
Mobile (4× CPU throttle): TBT 1,124 ms (poor), CLS 0.212 (needs-improvement), LCP 2.87 s (needs-improvement). Desktop all good. 8 long tasks on mobile load; heavy third-party layer (OneTrust, Adobe Target, Qualtrics, heap, Intercom). measurement — measurements.cwv · full table in §5
Impact
Mobile visitors — the restaurant/retail owners the copy targets — get a sluggish first interaction; INP-class failures correlate directly with bounce and lost sign-ups.
Fix
Defer/gate the third-party stack (consent-manager, analytics, survey, chat) until after first interaction; split the hydration bundle; keep hero video but set poster + explicit dimensions to cut CLS.

mwg:optimize-script-priority mwg:identify-heavy-scripts mwg:performance
F-004 P2 technical-seo

No structured data anywhere: zero JSON-LD on all five audited pages

Evidence
No Organization, Product, FAQPage, or BreadcrumbList entities; pricing and pos-systems render FAQ accordions with no FAQPage markup. check — check:jsonld
Impact
Search and answer engines cannot build an entity model of Clover; rich results (FAQ, product, org knowledge panel) are forfeited to competitors.
Fix
Ship Organization + WebSite JSON-LD site-wide, Product on device pages, FAQPage on pricing/pos-systems FAQs, BreadcrumbList on solution pages.
F-005 P2 technical-seo

Home <title> targets the Food & Beverage vertical; Open Graph absent site-wide

Evidence
“POS Solutions for Food & Beverage Businesses | Clover” on https://www.clover.com/ — the brand home competes with its own F&B solution page. og:title/description/image/type/url: none rendered on any audited page. check — check:title-missing
Impact
The most-linked page on the domain mis-declares its topic; every social/chat share of any page renders as a bare link with no card.
Fix
Set the home title to the brand + category head term (“Clover | POS Systems & Payments for Small Business”); emit the five core OG tags (+ twitter:card) per page from the CMS layer.
F-006 P2 technical-seo

Public sitemap ships 18 malformed URLs plus test/legacy pages

Evidence
18 <loc> values missing the path slash (e.g. https://www.clover.compos-systems/employees — an invalid host), plus /test-bundle-issue and /pos-systems/employee-management-old in the us/en sitemap; robots.txt declares no Sitemap directive. measurement — measurements.sitemapValid
Impact
Crawl budget burns on 404-class fetches and junk pages; the malformed hosts are dead ends that erode sitemap trust.
Fix
Fix the URL-join bug in sitemap generation, drop test/-old pages, add a Sitemap: directive to robots.txt.
F-007 P2 accessibility

Heading semantics broken in both directions across the site

Evidence
Home renders 2 H1s (“A Clover for every small business” + “Restaurant-grade hardware…”); a testimonial pull-quote is an H2; heading level-skips on all 5 pages (7 distinct skipped pairs: h1→h3, h2→h4, h1→h5, h2→h5). check — check:single-h1
Impact
Screen-reader heading navigation is unreliable (a quote announces as a section); document outline signals to search engines are noisy.
Fix
One H1 per page; demote the hardware section heading to H2; mark quotes as <blockquote>; close the h_→h_+2 skips in both template systems.
Guides
mwg:html
F-008 P2 conversion-focus

Three unexplained purchase paths compete in one header

Evidence
“Pricing”, “Shop systems”, a cart icon, and “Contact sales” coexist in the header (plus “Get Clover” in the hero) with no signal of which path suits whom; “Pricing” and “Shop systems” lead to different numbers. tension — T-nav-conflict
Impact
Self-serve vs assisted-sales confusion adds a decision cost at every page view; first-timers cannot predict where the money question gets answered.
Fix
Collapse to one commercial CTA pair — “Shop” (self-serve, with prices) and “Talk to sales” — and make Pricing a first-class nav peer rather than a utility link.
F-009 P2 accessibility

Video-backed sections have no legible fallback state

Evidence
White uppercase display text renders over a gray-to-white ground when the background video has not loaded — the captured fallback state dissolves the quote’s third line into white-on-white. Detector: contrast indeterminate over video (media ignored by resolver). Note: the stat-band gray-on-lime pair the detector flagged as washed-out actually computes 7.07:1 and passes AA — aesthetic only, not a failure. screenshot — home.png, testimonial + hardware sections (~y 6,100–8,300 on the 2880px capture) · Figure 3
Impact
Reduced-motion users, slow connections, and data-saver mobile see exactly this state; the brand’s loudest sections become unreadable for them.
Fix
Give every video section an opaque dark poster/background-color matched to the video’s luminance so text contrast never depends on the stream; verify the reduced-motion state renders the same scrim.
Guides
mwg:visually-stable-font-fallbacks
F-010 P2 content-llm-visibility

Answer-engine surface is thin: no llms.txt, soft-200 unknown routes, 250-word home, slogan headings

Evidence
/llms.txt returns the SPA shell as HTTP 200 (soft-404). Home carries 250 words of extractable prose; 18.8% of h2/h3 are question/topic-shaped — the rest are slogans (“Run the numbers”, “You bring the flavor…”). No structured data (F-004) compounds this. measurement — measurements.llmsTxt · detail in §6
Impact
AI answer engines summarizing “best POS for restaurants” have little citable Clover text to work with; competitors with FAQ schema and question-shaped headings get quoted instead.
Fix
Publish llms.txt; return real 404s on unknown paths; add an extractable what-it-is/who-it’s-for/pricing-anchor block to home; reshape section headings on commercial pages toward askable questions (keep slogans as display sublines).
F-011 P3 brand-expression

Logo variant set is partial and two palette colors are single-context

Evidence
No white/knockout wordmark captured for dark grounds; #004422 and #333333 appear as text only (T-color-imbalance ×2). tension — T-logo-variants
Impact
Dark-ground surfaces (footer, video heroes) improvise logo treatment; the palette carries dead weight.
Fix
Produce the knockout wordmark; decide drop-vs-expand for the two single-context colors in the redesign token layer.
F-012 P3 visual-hierarchy-craft

Footer legal text is set below readability floors

Evidence
Footer legal paragraphs at 1.29× line-height and 104–111 characters per line on every page (detector, 3 pages). check — detector:tight-leading+line-length
Impact
The disclosure text that qualifies the $450 offer (3-year contract) is effectively unreadable — a compliance-adjacent trust issue.
Fix
Set legal text at ≥1.5 line-height, ≤75ch measure; consider a “key terms” summary line next to promoted offers.
F-013 P3 brand-expression

Design-token layer is vestigial (one custom property site-wide)

Evidence
One CSS custom property across five pages; the visual system lives in compiled CSS-module class hashes (mAZF70It--…). tension — T-no-tokens
Impact
Every brand decision (like the template migration) is a rebuild instead of a token flip; slows the very consolidation F-002 needs.
Fix
Introduce a :root token layer (colors, type, radius, spacing) as part of the template consolidation.

Benchmarks

Three references the scoring leaned on.

Reference set

Square squareup.com

Same vertical, one system: a single accent (#006aff) reserved exclusively for CTAs/active states, one type family pair held from hero to footer, and a disciplined radius set — the consistency Clover splits across two template generations. Pricing answers in one screen.

GlossGenius glossgenius.com

Adjacent vertical (salon commerce). Also shadow-free and accent-led like Clover, but with a coherent 13→144px type scale and accent reserved almost exclusively for the one ask — proof the flat/acid-accent strategy works when the scale and CTA discipline hold.

refero style corpus refero.design (fintech/commerce)

Across best-in-class payment-platform sites, the recurring pattern is one hue + one display register + prices visible within one screen of the pricing URL — Clover has the first, half of the second, and not the third.

Uplift directions

Three pitches, each naming the findings it resolves.

A

“Tomorrow’s version of the site you have today.”

Same IA, the new-template system finished site-wide: header/CTA/footer tokens ported to pricing and pos-systems, one H1 per page, anchor prices on /pricing, JSON-LD + OG shipped, third-party scripts deferred (mobile TBT under 600ms).

Resolves F-001F-002F-003F-004F-005F-007F-008

B

“What if the whole site ran the numbers?”

Amplify the captured signature — the acid-lime stat band, Formula Condensed display, and real product-UI vignettes — into the compositional spine of every commercial page: stats, proof, and prices rendered as the brand’s own loud, flat, green system.

Resolves F-002F-001F-011F-013

C

“What if motion was part of the identity?” kinetic-display

Same IA as A; the brand’s existing ambient video language graduated into a kinetic-display register — condensed type that moves like the stat band reads, video always behind an opaque luminance-matched scrim (fixing the fallback failure), full prefers-reduced-motion fallback.
